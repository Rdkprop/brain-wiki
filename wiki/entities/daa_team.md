## Key Themes (June 2026)

1. **Team is short on buffer.** Most focus areas have thin or no named backup.
2. **Consumer needs a clearer two-person model.** Search & Discovery and Growth & Lifecycle are each large enough to justify dedicated ownership; shared capacity on Enquirer Experience risks accountability blur.
3. **Abhi is cross-pod.** Touching Agent, Sales Enablement, and Trust — sustainable only short-term.
4. **Trust Pod is least defined.** Analytics requirements still unclear; scope has net-new items.

---

## Headcount View

**Recommendation:** one additional senior flexible DA / product analyst.

**Rationale:**
- De-risks Consumer if Phuong's scope changes
- Gives Mounica a real partner (not just overflow support)
- Absorbs Trust / New Projects ambiguity
- Protects against leave / attrition / re-org movement
- Reduces overload on Abhi

**If no extra headcount approved:**
- Keep Payal as second Consumer DA but treat next 1-2 months as ramp, not full ownership
- Lock Phuong on Search & Discovery until VN direction is clear
- Abhi + Fangzi share MY and Trust themes short-term
- New Projects and Seller Solution remain buffer-managed
