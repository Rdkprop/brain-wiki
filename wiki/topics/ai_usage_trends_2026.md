# AI Usage Trends 2026

Research findings from "AI in the Wild" — a longitudinal study by Marc Zao-Sanders and Sara Biuk tracking real-world generative AI usage. Third annual edition, analysing 12,637 use cases from ~50,000 records collected March 2025–Feb 2026. Sources: Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, TikTok, YouTube, and articles.

## Agentic AI at Work

Autonomous agentic operations entered at #6. Current usage is experimental and small-scale — most examples involve note-to-action conversion (voice memos auto-transcribed then auto-routed to calendar, notes, or tasks). True end-to-end process automation remains rare.

This corroborates the [[analytics_agent]] trajectory: agentic AI is gaining real-world traction but is still maturing as a reliable replacement for existing workflows.

---

## AI at Work — The Messy Reality

63 of the top 100 use cases are explicitly work-related or dual-use. But workplace AI adoption is uneven:

- **Tailwinds:** LLM licenses, training sessions, C-suite encouragement
- **Headwinds:** IT governance, restricted LLM versions, reputational risk, fear of job loss, policy compliance anxiety

**Shadow usage is common.** Users report closing tickets 2x faster and getting praised in reviews — without disclosing AI use. Some have secretly built AI agents to handle 50% of their own jobs after management rejected their proposals.

**The productivity reality:** Most gains are modest — first drafts, summarising notes, explaining data, drafting templates. Genuinely transformational use cases are rare, early-stage, and concentrated in SMEs.

## Cognitive Risks — [[Thinkslop]]

The study introduces "thinkslop" — the lazy, sloppy thinking engendered by over-reliance on AI. At least a quarter of the top use cases involve AI doing some portion of human thinking (therapy, decision-making, email drafting, idea generation).

Three mechanisms that degrade thinking:
1. **Prompting before thinking** — firing off a prompt before fully reasoning through the problem
2. **Wholesale pasting** — taking AI output with minimal editing, producing "workslop"
3. **AI sycophancy** — AI lavishes praise, user stops refining too early

See [[thinkslop]] for the full concept and countermeasures.

## Emotional AI — Growing and High-Stakes

Therapy/companionship is #1 and growing fast (5% → 11% YoY). Related uses: relationship advice (#7), love lives (#17), reconciling disputes (#26).

People anthropomorphise AI — naming chatbots, assigning genders, experiencing grief when models update. High-profile cases of AI psychosis and AI romance show this is high-stakes territory. Clinicians caution that general-purpose AI is not a substitute for trained mental health professionals.
