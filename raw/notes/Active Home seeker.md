**Home Seekers** are users who, in the **past 30 days**, have:

- **≥ 3 Searches**, **AND**
- **≥ 4 Listing Detail Page Views (LDPVs)**

**Why this definition was chosen**

- Combines **intent (search)** and **engagement (views)**
- Less prone to contamination than a pure “50+ LDPVs” threshold
- Better aligns with real consumer behavior and downstream lead generation

**50+ LDPVs is treated as an _outcome / average_, not a qualification rule**
## **Scale of Home Seekers (Monthly)**

Based on shared distributions:

|Month|Home Seekers|
|---|---|
|Oct 2025|~240K|
|Nov 2025|~221K|
|Dec 2025|~209K|
|Jan 2026|~258K|
Home seekers represent a **minority of total visitors**, but are the **primary source of leads**.
## **Engagement Characteristics**

- **LDPVs per Home Seeker**
    - **Median:** ~18
    - **Average:** ~50 (right‑skewed by heavy users)
- **Leads per Home Seeker**
    - **Average:** ~0.8
    - **Median:** 1 (expected, given conversion sparsity)

Strong **right‑skewed distribution** observed:

- Small cohort shows **extreme LDPVs (hundreds to thousands)**
- Suspected influence from **bots, scrapers, or agents**
- Risk: Inflated baseline → unrealistic targets
## **Target‑Setting Approach (Decision)**

- **Use MEDIAN for engagement depth**
- **Use AVERAGE for leads**
**Agreed Directional Targets**

- Median LDPVs / Home Seeker: **18 → 22** (+20%)
- Avg Leads / Home Seeker: **0.8 → 1.0**
# KPIs 

## **Core KPIs 

### 1. **Home Seekers (Count)**

- Monthly unique users with **≥3 searches AND ≥4 LDPVs**
- Absolute number + MoM trend
### 2. **% Home Seekers of Total Visitors**

- Home Seekers ÷ Total Monthly Unique Visitors
- First‑order indicator of **demand depth**
### 3. **Median LDPVs per Home Seeker**

- Primary engagement‑depth KPI
- **Preferred over average** due to skew
### 4. **Average Leads per Home Seeker**

- Primary conversion KPI
- Tracks monetizable value per high‑intent user
## **Supporting / Diagnostic KPIs**

### 5. **LDPV Distribution (Home Seekers)**

- % of users in buckets:
    - 1–10, 11–20, 21–50, 51–100, 100+
- Helps spot **abnormal spikes**
### 6. **Leads Contribution Share**

- % of total leads coming from Home Seekers
- Expected to be **~90%+**
### 7. **Extreme Activity Monitor (Quality Guardrail)**

- % of Home Seekers with:
    - > 500 LDPVs
        
    - > 1000 LDPVs
        
- Flag for bot / scraper investigation
### 8. **Email Coverage Rate (High‑LDPV Users)**

- % of extreme‑LDPV users with valid emails
- Compared vs baseline Home Seeker population
## **Optional Segmentation ( Phase 2)**

- **Mid‑Intent vs High‑Intent Home Seekers**
- Based on higher search & view thresholds
- Useful for targeted experiments and funnel optimization
